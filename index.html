Method of Joints - Theory Guide

1. Basic Principles

A truss is in static equilibrium when every joint satisfies Σ Fx = 0 and Σ Fy = 0.

2. Tension vs Compression

Tension (T): Pulls AWAY from the pin joint (+ positive).

Compression (C): Pushes INTO the pin joint (- negative).

3. Zero-Force Members

Unloaded joints with 2 non-collinear members or 3 members with 2 collinear carry 0 force.
